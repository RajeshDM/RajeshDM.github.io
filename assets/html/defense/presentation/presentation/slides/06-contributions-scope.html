Thesis Contributions
Learning (Fully Observable)
GABAR
Graph Attention-Based Action Ranking
Fully observable classical planning. Learns to rank actions from small solved instances using action-centric graphs. Generalizes to problems up to 8x larger.
NeurIPS 2025
R. Mangannavar, A. Fern, S. Lee, P. Tadepalli
Learning (Partially Observable)
GammaZero
Graph-Based Learning for POMDP Planning
Transforms beliefs into uncertainty-aware graphs. Learned value and policy guide MCTS. Zero-shot generalization to 2-6x larger POMDP instances.
ICAPS 2026
R. Mangannavar, P. Tadepalli
Abstraction (Partially Observable)
HOO-POMDP
Hierarchical Object-Oriented POMDP Planning
Partially observable rearrangement. Three forms of abstraction make POMDP planning tractable for 10-20 objects across multi-room environments.
