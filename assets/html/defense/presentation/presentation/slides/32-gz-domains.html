Experimental Domains

4 POMDP benchmarks — partial observability, zero-shot generalization

LightDark(d)

1D localization. Must visit light region to reduce uncertainty before reaching goal.

Train: LD(5)
Test: LD(10)

RockSample(n,k)

n×n grid, k rocks of unknown quality. Noisy distance-dependent sensor. Up to 400M+ states.

Train: RS(5,5)–(10,10)
Test: RS(15,15)–(25,25)

MultiObjectSearch

Find k hidden objects on n×n grid. Range-limited sensor.

Train: MOS(3,2)–(4,3)
Test: MOS(6,4)–(8,6)

Rearrangement

Locate k objects, transport to goals. Perception + manipulation.

Train: RG(3,2)–(4,3)
Test: RG(6,4)–(8,5)
